Horizontal 16:9 cinematic wide shot. LOCKED-OFF STATIC CAMERA sitting in the grass, roughly 30cm above the ground, as though a phone had been propped upright in the stems and left running. 40mm lens, shallow depth of field on the extreme foreground. The camera never moves: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly, no shake, for the entire shot. Only the woman, the creature and the grass move.

FOREGROUND — Blades of green grass rise through the lower corners of the frame, close to the lens and far out of focus.

SUBJECT — A woman knight, back to camera. At the start she is close to the lens and large in frame, filling the lower third. Ivory hooded cloak to mid-calf. Mirror-polished silver plate armour. Chin-length black bob. A long straight silver sword hangs from her right hand, tip low. She walks away from the camera into knee-high wind-rippled grass, her feet hidden among the stems, and shrinks to about one sixth of the frame height before stopping dead centre.

CREATURE — Ahead of her, at first only a vast pale blue-grey mass in the fog, resolving as she recedes into a colossal dragon lying prone in the meadow, head resting on the ground, snout pointing right, eyes closed. Its head alone is five times her full height. Pearl-white and ice-blue hide. A broad field of opalescent dichroic scales across the neck and shoulder throwing prismatic pink, cyan, mint and gold glints. A saw-tooth ridge of tall bone-white dorsal spines runs down the neck and back. A fan of long pale spines frames the cheeks and jaw like a frill. Two thick bone-white horns sweep back from the crown, banded near the base with dark bronze metal cuffs. Two whip-thin bronze tendrils arc from behind each eye over the brow. Fine pebbled scales on the muzzle, pink nostril slits, a pink-flushed nose. An enormous folded membranous wing, semi-translucent pale grey-pink with visible finger bones, lies on the left. The far body dissolves into fog. Slow faint plumes of breath vapour drift from the nostrils into the cold air.

ACTION, in strict order —
(0-1.5 s) She walks away from the camera, shrinking, and the pale mass ahead of her resolves into the sleeping creature.
(1.5-4 s) She stops. Stillness. The dragon sleeps. Nothing happens. Only the grass and her cloak move in the wind.
(4-5.8 s) One vast amber eye opens — warm gold iris, vertical black slit pupil — and finds her. The head does not move.
(5.8-7 s) She raises the sword slowly out to her side.
(7-10 s) The head lifts off the ground, the grass beneath its jaw flattening and springing back, and begins to rotate toward her. The second eye opens. Hold.

LIGHT — Flat overcast daylight, fully diffuse, no shadows, no visible sun, cool blue-grey. Dense low ground fog. Flat pale blue-white sky. Soft bloom on the white scales.

STYLE — Photoreal cinematic, cool desaturated grade, teal-cyan shadows, lifted milky blacks, low contrast, heavy atmospheric perspective, fine 35mm grain, subtle vignette. Epic fantasy realism, live-action, not animated.
